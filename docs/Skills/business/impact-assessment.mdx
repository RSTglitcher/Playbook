# Impact Assessment & Financial Alignment

> **Operational Purpose:** Evaluate the economic, operational, and strategic consequences of proposed technical initiatives—quantifying financial return, classifying expenditure (OpEx vs CapEx), and measuring total cost of ownership (TCO) to justify engineering investment.

---

## 1. Context & Application Trigger

* **When to perform:** Prior to committing engineering capacity to new epics, when evaluating major architectural refactors, when selecting third-party SaaS/vendors, or during quarterly planning and resource allocation.
* **Inputs needed:** Proposed technical scope, estimated engineering effort (story points/sprints), cloud infrastructure estimates, vendor licensing models, and business success metrics.
* **Expected Output:** A completed Business Impact Assessment (BIA) document, financial classification (OpEx/CapEx), estimated ROI/payback period, and risk/cost-of-inaction analysis.

---

## 2. Standard Operating Procedure (Step-by-Step)

1. **Phase 1: Financial Classification & Budget Alignment**
   * Classify the initiative's capital and operational profile:
     * **CapEx (Capital Expenditure):** Creation of new software assets, long-term platform capabilities, or major architecture additions (capitalized and depreciated over time).
     * **OpEx (Operational Expenditure):** Recurring cloud run costs (AWS/GCP), SaaS licenses, routine maintenance, and ongoing operational support.
   * Calculate Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) across a 12–36 month horizon: $\textTCO = \textBuild Cost (CapEx) + \textRecurring Run Costs (OpEx)$.

2. **Phase 2: Direct & Indirect Impact Quantification**
   * Estimate **Direct Financial Return:** Cost reductions (e.g., reducing database compute or third-party API calls) or direct revenue enablement.
   * Estimate **Operational & Velocity Impact:** Time saved per sprint, reduction in manual toil (FTE hours), or improved developer throughput.
   * Quantify **Risk & Compliance Exposure:** Financial cost of potential SLA breach penalties, security compliance failures, or system downtime.

3. **Phase 3: Cost-of-Inaction & Opportunity Cost Analysis**
   * Evaluate the **Cost of Inaction (COI):** *What happens to system performance, operational spend, or market positioning if we do nothing for 6–12 months?*
   * Compare opportunity costs against competing engineering priorities to ensure maximum business return per engineering unit.

4. **Phase 4: Synthesis & Executive Decision Brief**
   * Draft a concise 1-page Impact Assessment brief summarizing effort vs financial/operational return.
   * Present options (e.g., *Option A: Full Build, Option B: Minimal Viable Patch, Option C: Do Nothing*) with explicit trade-offs.

## 3. Key Artifacts & Templates

### Business Impact Assessment (BIA) Template

```text
# Impact Assessment: [Initiative Name]

**Owner:** Technical Analyst / Lead  
**Target Window:** [Quarter / Fiscal Year]  
**Financial Classification:** [CapEx / OpEx / Hybrid]  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition
[Brief summary of the proposed change and its primary commercial or operational payback]

## 2. Financial Breakdown & TCO

| Category | Cost Item | Expense Type | Estimated Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Build Effort** | 3 Sprints (4 Engineers) | CapEx | $XX,XXX |
| **Infrastructure** | AWS Aurora Auto-scaling | OpEx (Annual) | $X,XXX/yr |
| **Third-Party** | Vendor API Subscription | OpEx (Annual) | $X,XXX/yr |
| **Total (Year 1 TCO)** | | | **$XX,XXX** |

## 3. Value & Impact Drivers
- 💰 **Cost Savings:** Reduces monthly cloud infrastructure spend by $X,XXX ($XX,XXX annually).
- ⚡ **Operational Velocity:** Automates manual data transformation, saving ~15 FTE hours/sprint.
- 🛡️ **Risk Mitigation:** Eliminates single point of failure in payment pipeline, protecting ~$XXX,XXX in daily transactional throughput.

## 4. Cost of Inaction (COI) & Trade-offs
- **If Delayed 6 Months:** Storage costs will scale non-linearly by +35%; risk of exceeding $P_{99}$ latency SLAs during peak sales events.

```

---

## 4. Quality Checklist & Verification

* [ ] Technical expenditure is correctly classified into **CapEx** (asset creation) or **OpEx** (ongoing run cost)
* [ ] Financial calculations account for both initial build cost and long-term operating costs (TCO)
* [ ] Non-financial benefits (velocity, risk reduction) are quantified with concrete metrics
* [ ] The Cost of Inaction (COI) is explicitly defined alongside the proposed solution

---

:::warning

## Pitfalls & Edge Cases

* **Common Mistake:** Focus solely on initial engineering build costs while ignoring recurring cloud or licensing OpEx, resulting in unexpected budget overruns down the line.
* **Pro Tip:** Frame technical debt or architectural improvements in terms of **Risk & Cost of Inaction**. Executives respond far better to "Ignoring this will increase cloud spend by 25% and breach SLAs" than "The code is messy."

:::
