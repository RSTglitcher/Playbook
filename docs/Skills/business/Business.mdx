# Business & Impact Assessment

:::tip

**Domain Philosophy:** Technical analysis without business context is incomplete. The **Business** domain bridges engineering execution with commercial reality—evaluating the financial impact, resource allocation (OpEx vs. CapEx), and operational return of every technical initiative.

:::

---

## 1. What It Means to Focus on Impact

While **Analysis** zeroes in on *problem framing and system behavior*, **Business** evaluates the *economic, operational, and strategic consequences* of tackling or ignoring a problem.

### The Impact Spectrum
1. **Financial Impact:** Evaluating cost reduction, revenue enablement, and expenditure classification (**CapEx** vs. **OpEx**).
2. **Operational Impact:** Measuring efficiency gains, developer velocity, and reductions in toil.
3. **Risk & Compliance Impact:** Quantifying exposure from SLA breaches, security vulnerabilities, or data loss.

---

## 2. Financial Alignment: CapEx vs. OpEx Framework

Understanding expenditure categories allows technical decisions to align with corporate financial strategy:

| Expenditure Type | Technical Scope Examples | Business Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CapEx (Capital Expenditure)** | • Building new core platform capabilities<br/>• Major architectural modernization<br/>• R&D / new product features | Creates long-term capitalized assets; depreciated over time on financial balance sheets. |
| **OpEx (Operational Expenditure)** | • Infrastructure/cloud run costs (AWS/GCP)<br/>• SaaS licenses & third-party APIs<br/>• Routine maintenance & defect fixes | Immediate operational cost; directly impacts short-term operating margins and profitability. |

## 4. Impact Assessment Checklist

Before committing engineering capacity to a initiative, verify:

- [ ] Is the proposal classified as **CapEx** (asset creation) or **OpEx** (ongoing run cost)?
- [ ] What is the expected financial return (e.g., cloud cost savings, SLA penalty avoidance, user expansion)?
- [ ] What is the operational cost of *inaction* (doing nothing)?
- [ ] Have ongoing infrastructure and licensing costs been factored into the total cost of ownership (TCO)?
