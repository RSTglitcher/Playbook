**Domain Philosophy:** True delivery excellence is measured not by how fast you move, but by how **consistently and confidently** you land commitments. Predictability builds trust with stakeholders; quality gates ensure every release can be deployed without anxiety.

:::

## 1. What It Means to Focus on Predictability & Confidence

While **Analysis** defines the problem, **Business** evaluates its impact, and **Development** writes the code, **Delivery** ensures that work lands in production on time, with high fidelity, and without last-minute operational surprises.

### Predictability over Speed, Confidence over Output

* ❌ **The Output Illusion:** Maximizing story point velocity often leads to erratic release dates, bloated work-in-progress (WIP), and high anxiety during production deployments.
* ✅ **Predictability & Confidence Mindset:** Right-sizing scope, managing queue bottlenecks, establishing clear quality criteria (**DoR** and **DoD**), and maintaining a release pipeline so reliable that deployments become non-events.

## 2. The Pillars of Confident Delivery

To achieve sustainable delivery confidence, focus on four core operational pillars:

```text
┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐
│  Definition of Ready    │ ───► │   WIP Constraint &      │
│  Unambiguous DoR gates  │      │   Flow Management       │
└─────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘
             │                                │
             ▼                                ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────────┐
│  Definition of Done     │ ◄─── │  Predictable Cadence &  │
│  Automated QA & DoD     │      │  Scope Management       │
└─────────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────────┘

```

1. **Upfront Clarity (DoR):** Eliminating ambiguity before work enters a sprint so engineers don't hit preventable mid-sprint blockers.
2. **Controlled Work-In-Progress (WIP):** Limiting open tickets to prevent context-switching and accelerate time-to-completion.
3. **Built-In Quality (DoD):** Enforcing strict Definition of Done criteria (automated tests, staging sign-offs, telemetry) to deploy with zero hesitation.
4. **Transparent Risk Management:** Surface delivery risks and cross-team dependencies early while trade-offs can still be made predictably.

## 3. Delivery Confidence Indicators

Track these stability indicators to measure team health and delivery reliability:

| Metric | Delivery Purpose | Operational Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commitment Ratio** | Measures sprint predictability (Delivered Points vs Committed Points). | Target: $85\%–95\%$ consistency across consecutive sprints. |
| **Cycle Time Variance** | Measures how reliably ticket duration can be estimated. | Low variance indicates stable, well-bounded work items. |
| **Change Failure Rate (CFR)** | Measures production confidence and test coverage effectiveness. | Target: $< 5\%$ of deployments require patch/rollback. |
| **Blocker Age** | Tracks how rapidly flow bottlenecks are resolved. | Unblock critical WIP in $< 24hours$. |

## 5. Delivery Readiness Checklist

Before committing to a release cycle or milestone, verify:

* [ ] Are all candidate items vetted against the **Definition of Ready (DoR)**?
* [ ] Is team capacity modeled on historical completion trends rather than ideal scenarios?
* [ ] Are cross-squad or third-party dependencies identified and scheduled in advance?
* [ ] Does every ticket have explicit, testable criteria satisfying the **Definition of Done (DoD)**?
