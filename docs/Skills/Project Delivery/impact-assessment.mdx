# 📘 Impact Assessment & Financial Alignment

:::tip

## Impact Assessment & Financial Alignment

**What is it?** 
A structured evaluation skill used to assess the economic, operational, and strategic consequences of proposed technical initiatives—quantifying financial returns, classifying expenditure types (OpEx vs CapEx), and calculating total cost of ownership (TCO) to justify engineering investment.

#### 📚 Related Theory & Practice References
* Theory — Software economics, capital allocation principles, and technical debt compounding models.
* Practice — Architecture estimation, roadmap planning, and Jira epic sizing.
:::

## 🧭 When is it Used?

| When to Use | When Not to Use |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Prior to committing engineering capacity to major new product epics or complex architectural refactors. • When evaluating third-party SaaS vendors, cloud migration projects, or infrastructure scale-ups. • During quarterly roadmap planning and resource allocation cycles. | • Handling routine bug fixes, minor code optimizations, or standard daily sprint tasks. • Emergency production incident triage requiring immediate operational mitigation. |

---

## 💡 What Value Does It Bring?

* **Justifies Engineering Investment:** Replaces subjective feature requests with rigorous commercial ROI and risk mitigation metrics.
* **Prevents Budget Overruns:** Accounts for long-term operational costs (TCO) rather than focusing solely on initial build effort.
* **Improves Prioritization:** Clarifies opportunity costs and the "Cost of Inaction," ensuring engineering teams work on initiatives that deliver maximum business value.

---

## 🧠 Core Concepts

| Concept / Mechanism | Plain English Explanation | Execution Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CapEx vs. OpEx Classification** | CapEx (Capital Expenditure) covers asset creation and long-term capability building (depreciated over time); OpEx (Operational Expenditure) covers recurring cloud run costs and SaaS subscriptions. | Ensures compliance with financial accounting standards and corporate tax strategies. |
| **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** | Calculating the complete lifetime cost of an initiative, combining initial build effort, maintenance, and long-term hosting/licensing fees over a 12-36 month horizon. | Prevents teams from building "cheap" solutions that become prohibitively expensive to operate. |
| **Cost of Inaction (COI)** | Quantifying the financial, operational, or security cost of *not* doing the project for 6 to 12 months. | Provides powerful leverage when convincing executive stakeholders to prioritize technical debt or infrastructure modernization. |

---

## 📋 Common Patterns

1. **Phase 1: Scope & Financial Classification** — Outline the proposed engineering scope, estimate team sprint effort, and categorize expenditures into CapEx and OpEx buckets.
2. **Phase 2: Impact Quantification & TCO Modeling** — Calculate 12-to-36-month TCO, direct cost savings, FTE velocity improvements, and risk mitigation value.
3. **Phase 3: Cost-of-Inaction & Decision Brief Synthesis** — Summarize findings into a 1-page executive brief outlining alternative options and clear trade-offs.

## 🛠️ Practical Tips & Templates

> **Template / Artifact Example:** Business Impact Assessment (BIA) Confluence Template.

```markdown
# Impact Assessment: [Initiative Name]

**Owner:** Technical Analyst / Lead  
**Target Window:** [Quarter / Fiscal Year]  
**Financial Classification:** [CapEx / OpEx / Hybrid]  

## 1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition
[Brief summary of the proposed change and its primary commercial or operational payback]

## 2. Financial Breakdown & TCO
| Category | Cost Item | Expense Type | Estimated Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Build Effort** | 3 Sprints (4 Engineers) | CapEx | $XX,XXX |
| **Infrastructure** | Cloud Hosting Auto-scaling | OpEx (Annual) | $X,XXX/yr |
| **Total (Year 1 TCO)** | | | **$XX,XXX** |

## 3. Value & Impact Drivers
- 💰 **Cost Savings:** Reduces monthly cloud infrastructure spend by $X,XXX annually.
- ⚡ **Operational Velocity:** Automates manual data pipeline, saving ~15 FTE hours/sprint.

## 4. Cost of Inaction (COI) & Trade-offs
- **If Delayed 6 Months:** Storage costs will scale non-linearly by +35%; risk of breaching latency SLAs during peak events.

```

:::warning

### Pitfalls & Edge Cases

* **Common Mistake:** Focusing solely on initial engineering build costs while ignoring recurring cloud or licensing OpEx, leading to unexpected budget overruns.
* **Pro Tip:** Frame technical debt or infrastructure improvements in terms of *Risk and Cost of Inaction*. Executives respond far better to financial risk quantification than abstract code cleanliness arguments.
:::
